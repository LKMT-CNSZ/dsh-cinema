## Cut

7.40 s: match cut on the horizon. W1's final pitch and W2's pitch are the same (`tan(pitch) = -0.0647`), so the horizon lands
within a few pixels; the frame is 1.8× closer and 25 % longer in focal length. The wind does not restart at the cut - it is
the same wind, at 7.4 s of film time.

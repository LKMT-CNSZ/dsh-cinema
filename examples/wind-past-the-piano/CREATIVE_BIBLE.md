# CREATIVE BIBLE — 《风经过钢琴》 / Wind Past the Piano

## The film in one sentence

A grand piano stands alone in a wide field at the end of the day; the wind arrives, and the piano's strings answer it, and a
woman who is not playing stands beside it and lets the sound happen.

## The place

A worked field, dry late autumn, a treeline at the far edge, one house too far away to live in, one bare tree. Nothing here
is maintained. The land is flat enough that the horizon is a true line and wide enough that a single object cannot be
explained by anything near it - which is the point of putting a piano in it.

## Weather and light

Overcast breaking to low sun in the late afternoon. One sun direction for the whole film: `[-0.70, 0.235, 0.67]`, about 13°
above the horizon, so shadows are long and the light comes from the left and behind the subject. Sky bounce is cool. A thin
cloud thins the sun by a few percent over the film; it never changes direction.

## What it is about

The sound outlives the player. Nobody touches the piano; the wind does it, and the film lets that be enough. The figure is
present as a witness, not a performer.

## The route

**2.5D.** Four painted plates (treeline, house, tree, foreground grass) placed at depths in a metric world, plus local 3D for
the two things whose proportions must survive any angle: the piano and the figure. The ground is the local-3D ground plane,
because a painted ground cannot keep its perspective under a dolly.

## Decisions made before the shot list

| decision | answer |
|---|---|
| route | 2.5D plates + local 3D piano, figure, ground, shadows |
| where the camera can stand | on the field's south edge, 8 m from the piano, 1.5 m high |
| sun | one direction, 13° elevation, from the left and behind |
| wind | steady, gusting at 4.7 s + 11.3 s, from the west; grass answers first, branches ~0.2 s later, clouds never notice |
| depth planes | 3 m (grass) · 8-9 m (piano, figure) · 14.5 m (tree) · 26 m (house) · 88 m (treeline) |
| horizon | 0.39 H, matched across the cut |
| emptiness | 0.35-0.45 of the frame may be sky or open field; the field is the subject's isolation, not a missing set |
| what is absent | no other people, no road, no weather drama, no music, no narration |

## What it refuses

- **No performance.** The figure does not sit down, play, turn or leave. She stands, and her coat moves.
- **No score.** The piano's sound is the wind in its strings - a physical event, not a cue.
- **No climax.** The film starts in the wind and ends in the wind; the only change is that the sound has been heard.
- **No surreal props.** The piano in a field is the surreal element, and it earns its place by being the subject. Nothing
  floats, no animal behaves strangely, no light comes from nowhere.

## The one image the film must have

A wide field, the piano small in it, the treeline behind, and enough air above that the piano looks like the only object in
the world. If that frame works, the film works; everything else is the wind.

## What would make it fail

1. A house that reads as a box, or a tree that reads as a stick with blobs - the panorama would become a diagram.
2. A foreground of flat grass - the near plane is where the frame proves it is painted with care.
3. A figure that acts, or a face that is drawn badly.
4. Camera movement with no reason: the dolly must be doing the one thing - slowly taking the piano out of the landscape it
   is lost in.
